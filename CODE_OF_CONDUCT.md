# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions
or elsewhere, is expected to be respectful and constructive.

Specifically:

- Be welcoming to newcomers and patient with questions.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks and sustained
  disruption are not tolerated.

Reports of unacceptable behaviour can be sent privately to the maintainers
(see the repository's contact details). Reports are handled confidentially,
and maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments, or
ban participants, in response to behaviour that breaches this document.
